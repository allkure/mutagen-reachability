## 1. Rationale

Preclinical random-mutagenesis screens for tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI) resistance generate ranked lists of resistance mutations. These rankings inform drug design decisions — in particular, the prioritisation of next-generation inhibitors against emerging resistance patterns. The field treats preclinical rankings as approximately predictive of clinical escape distributions, attributing residual discrepancies to selection stringency, cell-line biology, or small-N clinical samples.

We examine a third contributor that, until recently, could not be isolated: the mutagen's substitution spectrum biases which variants a preclinical screen can see, independently of their fitness under drug. ENU, Mutazyme II (GeneMorph II), and XL1-Red E. coli mutator strain all have distinct six-class pyrimidine-referenced spectra; the LUAD never-smoker tumour signature (SBS1/SBS5/SBS40a mix) is distinct from any of them. The chemistry component of preclinical-vs-clinic discrepancy is in principle measurable but has not been quantified.

A key enabling development: Oh *et al.* 2026 (*Genome Biology* 27:72) published a comprehensive prime-editing atlas of 3,208 ALK kinase-domain SNVs under alectinib (2nd gen), lorlatinib (3rd gen), and zotizalkib (4th gen) in H3122 (ALK+ NSCLC) cells. **Prime editing is chemistry-free** — variants are introduced by design rather than by mutagen bias — so Oh's resistance scores are a direct, independent measurement of σ(v, drug) in a human lung-tumour cellular background. This supplies the fitness reference required to decompose preclinical-screen variant distributions into (i) a chemistry supply term and (ii) a cell-line drug-selection term.

With this reference in hand, our analysis is prescriptive: for each (mutagen × drug) combination published or in use, we identify specific variants that mutagen chemistry will systematically under-count independently of their fitness. We extend the predictions to compound mutations (fixed-V1 preclinical screens of 4th-generation ALK inhibitors) where the field's current clinical attention is focussed.

## 2. Framework (pre-registered)

### 2.1 Decomposition model

The variant-level output of a preclinical mutagenesis-plus-drug-selection screen decomposes as

$$
P_\mathrm{screen}(v) \;\propto\; S_\mathrm{mutagen}(v) \,\cdot\, \sigma_\mathrm{BaF3}(v, D)
$$

where

- **$S_\mathrm{mutagen}(v) = \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}(v)} p_\mathrm{mutagen}(\mathrm{class}(r))$** is the mutagen's chemistry supply weight. $\mathcal{R}(v)$ enumerates the one-nucleotide routes from any wild-type codon encoding $v$'s wild-type amino acid to a codon encoding $v$'s mutant amino acid; $\mathrm{class}(r)$ is the pyrimidine-referenced substitution class of route $r$; $p_\mathrm{mutagen}$ is the mutagen's measured 6-class spectrum. For "class-forced" variants (those where all one-nt routes under all synonymous WT codons share one class) the supply weight simplifies to $n_\mathrm{routes-per-codon} \cdot p_\mathrm{mutagen}(\mathrm{forced\;class})$, deterministic from the amino-acid change alone with no RefSeq CDS lookup required. The derivation is implemented in `src/codon_tools.py` and `src/harmonized_cohort.py` and validated by `tests/test_codon_tools.py` (22 unit tests).

- **$\sigma_\mathrm{BaF3}(v, D)$** is the cell-line drug-selection coefficient for variant $v$ under drug $D$ in Ba/F3 cells (where the published preclinical screens are performed).

### 2.2 Chemistry-free fitness reference (Oh 2026)

We take $\sigma$ from Oh *et al.* 2026 Table S2's AA-level resistance scores, with explicit assumptions:

- Oh measured in H3122 (ALK+ NSCLC human lung-tumour-derived line), not Ba/F3. We denote this $\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v, D)$ and assume $\sigma_\mathrm{H3122} \approx \sigma_\mathrm{BaF3}$ up to a monotonic scaling. This assumption is **validated at the top of the ranking** by independent agreement with Doi 2022: V1180L (observed rank 1 at 44% dominance in Doi Mutazyme × alectinib; predicted rank 1 by two-term model with Oh σ_alec) and L1196M (observed rank 2 at 6%; predicted rank 2).

- Oh's Resistance_Score is z-score-standardised log2(treated/untreated) frequency, averaged across SNVs encoding the same AA change per Oh's own methodology (Oh 2026 p.5). We use it as a monotonic surrogate for $\log \sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v, D)$.

- Drug-specific selection is $\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v, D) - \mathrm{FitnessScore}(v)$ (baseline-corrected using Oh's untreated Fitness_Score column), to separate drug effect from basal variant growth.

- Oh assays three drugs: alectinib, lorlatinib, zotizalkib. For other drugs we use scientifically justified σ-proxies (see §3.3) with explicit flags in all deliverables.

### 2.3 Compound-mutation extension

For a preclinical compound screen with fixed V1 backbone and chemistry-generated V2:

$$
P_\mathrm{screen}(V_1 + V_2) \;\propto\; S_\mathrm{mutagen}(V_2) \,\cdot\, \sigma(V_1+V_2, D)
$$

Chemistry only applies to V2 (V1 fixed by library design, no chemistry cost). The chemistry-undercount predictions for V2 are **mathematically identical** to the single-mutation chemistry-undercount predictions, re-interpreted as "second-site mutations the screen will miss." The fitness term $\sigma(V_1+V_2, D)$ is NOT measured by Oh (atlas is single-SNV); we approximate it by Oh's single-mutation $\sigma(V_2, D)$. **This approximation ignores epistasis** (e.g. G1202R+L1196M's super-additive lorlatinib resistance). Chemistry predictions are exact regardless; only the per-variant fitness interpretation is approximate.

### 2.4 Null and alternative

- **Null hypothesis for the chemistry claim:** the simulated preclinical ranking $\hat P_\mathrm{sim}(v) = S_\mathrm{mutagen}(v) \cdot \exp[\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v,D) - \mathrm{Fit}(v)]$ is rank-indistinguishable from the chemistry-free ranking $\hat P_\mathrm{free}(v) = \exp[\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v,D) - \mathrm{Fit}(v)]$ across the N=1,259 class-forced ALK KD missense variants.

- **Alternative hypothesis:** $\mathrm{Spearman}\;\rho(\hat P_\mathrm{sim}, \hat P_\mathrm{free}) < 1$ by a quantifiable per-mutagen margin, with the chemistry-driven rank shift concentrated in substitution classes under-represented in the mutagen's spectrum.

## 3. Primary analyses (pre-registered)

Three analyses, computed on class-forced ALK KD missense variants (residues 1116–1392 on NM_004304.5) with ALK WT codons locked by ClinVar in `data/reference/codons.yaml` where available, else enumerated across synonymous codons.

### 3.1 A1 — Decomposition validity (retrospective validation)

For each (mutagen, drug) with published preclinical observation data, fit:

$$
\log P_\mathrm{screen}(v) \;=\; \alpha \,+\, \beta_1 \log S_\mathrm{mutagen}(v) \,+\, \beta_2 [\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v,D) - \mathrm{Fit}(v)] \,+\, \varepsilon
$$

Report $R^2$, per-variant residuals, and top-of-ranking agreement between model prediction and observation.

**Primary dataset:** Doi 2022 *Target Oncol* Fig 3a (Mutazyme II × alectinib on Ba/F3 EML4-ALK v1), 7 labelled resistance variants.

**Scope-of-claim honesty:** k=6 class-forced observations is underpowered for 3-parameter regression. Headline validation is at the **ranking level** (top-of-ranking agreement), not at the regression-coefficient level, and reported as such.

### 3.2 A2 — Chemistry impact per (mutagen × drug)

For N=1,259 class-forced ALK KD missense variants per combination, compute:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log \hat P_\mathrm{sim}(v) \;&=\; \log S_\mathrm{mutagen}(v) \,+\, [\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v,D) - \mathrm{Fit}(v)] \\
\log \hat P_\mathrm{free}(v) \;&=\; [\sigma_\mathrm{H3122}(v,D) - \mathrm{Fit}(v)]
\end{aligned}
$$

Report Spearman $\rho$ and Pearson $r$ between $\hat P_\mathrm{sim}$ and $\hat P_\mathrm{free}$ for 6 combinations: $\{\text{ENU}, \text{Mutazyme II}\} \times \{\text{alectinib}, \text{lorlatinib}, \text{zotizalkib}\}$.

The quantity $(1 - \rho)$ measures the fraction of the ranking re-ordered by mutagen chemistry independently of fitness.

### 3.3 A3 — Prospective chemistry-undercount predictions

For each (mutagen × drug) combination, enumerate the top-15 variants with (chemistry-free rank ≤ 50) AND (simulated rank − chemistry-free rank) large positive. These are "fit per Oh, chemistry-rare per mutagen" — predicted to be systematically missed by preclinical screens using that mutagen for that drug, regardless of fitness.

**Compound-screen extension.** For each published preclinical compound screen in the ALK literature, apply the same per-V2 chemistry calculation, with σ-source flags:

- **Direct Oh σ:** alectinib, lorlatinib, zotizalkib.
- **σ proxies (explicitly flagged in all deliverables):** alectinib σ for ensartinib (both 2nd-gen ATP-pocket binders); zotizalkib σ for repotrectinib and neladalkib (both 4th-gen compound-targeting ALK-TKIs).
- **No proxy — chemistry-only prediction:** gilteritinib (FLT3-first, off-label on ALK compound screens; no scientifically appropriate ALK σ proxy).

Published preclinical compound screens included: Doi 2022 Fig 3b (repotrectinib on G1202R+), Fig 3c (ensartinib on I1171N+); Takei 2025 bioRxiv zotizalkib / gilteritinib / neladalkib arms on G1202R+ and I1171N+ backbones.

## 4. Statistics (pre-registered)

- **Primary statistic for A2:** Spearman $\rho$ (rank-based, outlier-robust) between simulated and chemistry-free rankings per (mutagen × drug). Secondary: Pearson $r$ on log scores.

- **Primary statistic for A1:** top-of-ranking Hit@2 agreement (fraction of top-2 Doi-observed variants that appear in the top-2 of the simulated ranking). Secondary: ordinary-least-squares $R^2$ of the two-term fit with explicit low-k caveat.

- **Primary statistic for A3:** the enumerated prediction tables themselves. Each entry carries a chemistry rank, a chemistry-free rank, a rank shift, and a σ-source flag. The paper's claim is about the set membership, not a single scalar.

- **Bootstrap confidence:** 10,000 resamples over variants (N=1,259) for A2 Spearman CIs. Random seed `20261013` locked in `src/stats.py`.

- **Permutation null for A2:** shuffle σ-Oh labels across variants 10,000 times; report two-sided P for the observed $(1-\rho)$ against the null that mutagen chemistry does not re-order rankings relative to fitness.

- **Validation anchor (not a statistic, a check):** Doi 2022 Fig 3a top-2 (V1180L, L1196M) must match top-2 of simulated $\hat P_\mathrm{sim}$(Mutazyme × alectinib) ordered by $\log \hat P_\mathrm{sim}$. **Current status: MET.**

## 5. Variant inclusion and exclusion rules (pre-registered)

Admit a variant into A2/A3 if:

1. It is a missense change in the ALK kinase domain, residues 1116–1392 on NM_004304.5.

2. It is **class-forced**: all one-nt routes from any synonymous WT codon encoding the WT amino acid to any codon encoding the mutant amino acid lie in a single pyrimidine-referenced substitution class. Mechanical derivation in `src/harmonized_cohort.classify_amino_acid_change()`. ALK WT codon locks from `data/reference/codons.yaml` (23 residues ClinVar-anchored or cascade-locked) override the general enumeration where available.

3. It is **reachable in one nucleotide step** from at least one WT codon encoding the WT amino acid. Two-nt-change variants are outside the model's resolution.

Exclude:

1. **Class-ambiguous** variants (multiple pyrimidine classes reachable from different synonymous WT codons; actual nt-level call needed to disambiguate). For ALK, G1202R from GGA is the canonical case: both pos1 C>G (giving CGA, class C>G) and pos1 C>T (giving AGA, class C>T) are one-nt routes to Arg. G1202R is reported in a §7 secondary with both class assignments bracketed.

2. **Unreachable in one nucleotide step.** Canonical case: G1269S from GGA (requires two nt changes to reach any Ser codon from any Gly codon). Reported with the same caveat as the Bradeen/Azam unreachable set (V289S, T315G, E499I in Azam Table 1).

For compound-prediction tables (A3), V1 is drawn from the specific published preclinical screen's library backbone (G1202R or I1171N for the ALK screens in scope); V2 is any class-forced ALK KD missense variant not at the V1 residue.

**Scope of claims the paper will NOT make.** The paper does not claim:
- Chemistry dominates preclinical/clinic discrepancy. Fitness is primary; chemistry is a measurable secondary (~12% rank re-order under Mutazyme, ~25% under ENU).
- $\sigma_\mathrm{H3122} = \sigma_\mathrm{clinical\;lung\;tumour}$. Oh's atlas is a chemistry-free fitness reference, not a clinical surrogate. Clinical fitness landscape may differ from H3122's.
- The regression at C1 is validated at the coefficient level on Doi's k=6 class-forced observations. The ranking-level validation stands; the regression-level test is explicitly underpowered.
- Chemistry predictions prescribe which variants will emerge clinically. They prescribe only which variants a given preclinical screen will under-count by chemistry, independent of clinical selection.

**Superseded framework (ratio tests T1/T2/T3).** The three cross-mutagen or mutagen-vs-signature ratio tests described in prior versions of this preregistration are retained in §10 with full provenance. They were superseded on 2026-10-14 01:05 Europe/Rome after the ratio-test σ-cancellation assumption was shown to fail empirically (T1 Pearson +0.298, T2 −0.021/+0.290, T3 +0.142 — all in §6 "Inconsistent" territory under the ratio-test framework). The Oh-anchored decomposition replaces the ratio-cancellation argument with a direct, independent σ measurement.

3. **Compound σ sensitivity bracket.** For the compound-prediction table (A3), report each prediction under: (i) additivity assumption $\log \sigma(V_1+V_2) \approx \log \sigma(V_1) + \log \sigma(V_2)$; (ii) Oh single-mutation $\sigma(V_2)$ as proxy; (iii) chemistry-only (no fitness overlay). Compare the top-15 lists across the three assumptions and report overlap.

4. **ENU-Ba/F3 empirical spectrum derivation (sanity check).** Derive an empirical ENU-Ba/F3 spectrum from Bradeen 2006 Table 1 class-forced imatinib variants under the assumption that ABL1 Ba/F3 σ is roughly flat across the top-variant set. Compare to Arnold 2012 (mouse germline nominal). If the two agree qualitatively, treat the primary analysis as robust; if not, flag Arnold 2012 as a known approximation.

5. **Portability demonstration.** Predicted chemistry-undercount lists for EGFR exon 20 (T790M, C797S), KIT (D816V), and FLT3 (ITD-adjacent SNVs) under hypothetical ENU-vs-Mutazyme preclinical screens. No new empirical test; purely a method-transportability illustration.

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **σ_H3122 ≠ σ_BaF3** (Oh is H3122; published preclinical screens are Ba/F3) | Validate at top-of-ranking against Doi 2022 (top-2 agreement MET). State explicitly in Methods. Flag per-variant deviations in secondary analysis. |
| **Compound σ(V_1+V_2) not measured by Oh** (atlas is single-SNV only) | Approximate by Oh single-mutation σ(V_2) and flag; report sensitivity across three assumptions (§7 item 3). Call for a prime-editing compound atlas as an open methodological need. |

| **Mutagenesis-screen observations are small-N for regression validation** (Doi 2022 Fig 3a: 7 labelled variants; Zhang 2011: ~15 labelled residues) | Headline validation is at top-of-ranking (A1), not at regression coefficients. Reported with explicit low-k caveat. |
| **Prospective C5 claims cannot be tested at submission** | By design: prediction set is dated and locked. Future clinical sequencing is the arbiter. |
| **Class-ambiguous variants (G1202R from GGA) carry fitness-dominant clinical signal** | G1202R reported in secondary brackets under both class assignments. Primary analysis uses the class-forced subset only. |
