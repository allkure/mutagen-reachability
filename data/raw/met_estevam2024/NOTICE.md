# Attribution — MET kinase-domain DMS

The files in this folder (`met_dms_long.csv`, `met_dms_wide.csv`,
`met_dms_meta.json`) are derived long-format tables reformatted from
the raw deep mutational scan published by Estevam et al.

- **Primary publication:** Estevam et al., 2024 (`estevam2024` in
  `paper/refs.bib`; cited in `paper/paper.tex` §Datasets and §MET).
- **Source repository:** https://github.com/fraser-lab/MET_kinase_Inhibitor_DMS
- **Source licence:** MIT License (permits redistribution with
  attribution; the source repository's `LICENSE` file carries the
  original copyright notice).

The reformatting script that produced these tables from the Fraser
lab data is `scripts/extract_met_dms.py`.
